What is a canary

A canary is in a sense like a deadman's switch. a way to provide information to the user in the case of inaction. the inaction in this case would be me dying unexpectedly. due to the canary failing to update, readers of my website would be indirectly informed of my death with no action required from my side. just gotta make sure to update it regularly lol
